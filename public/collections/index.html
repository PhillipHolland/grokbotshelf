Find by job is for a visitor who knows the task and does not know the bot. A category page lists every template under one shelf label. A job page is the short list for one decision, such as dinner, a claim, or a scam text. Start on the card whose name matches the thing you are trying to finish. Each hub names a few bots to open first, then the rest of that job.

The order is the editors' order. The shelf has no install counts, so nothing here is ranked by popularity or by bookmarks on X. Import is one tap on the bot's own page, and the template is free. If the job is not listed, use the category pages or search on the home shelf.

Questions

When should I use Find by job?
When you know the task, such as dinner or a claim, and you do not know which bot to open. Each card is a short list for that job.
How do I pick a bot on a job page?
Read Start here first. Those are the editors' picks, in order. The shelf has no install counts, so this is not a popularity rank.
How is a job page different from a category?
A category lists every template with that label. A job page keeps only the bots chosen for that task, and links the category when you want the wider set.
